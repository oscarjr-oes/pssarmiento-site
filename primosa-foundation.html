Primosa Foundation, Inc.

In the tradition of the entrepreneurial Sarmientos, service to others has always been at the core of their values. In this third generation of the family, they express this by taking care of the communities around them — providing access to tools and education, and creating opportunities for entrepreneurial success.

Entrepreneurial success is a good measure of how much value you add to the lives of others. Being in a position to give back, the foundation is excited to create win-win opportunities for itself and the communities it belongs to.

Goals & values

  • Improve communities that PS Group is part of through sustainable development, access to tools and education, skills development, and entrepreneurship opportunities.
  • Create fertile ground not just for job creation, but for entrepreneurship within the community — businesses owned by community members creating more jobs.
  • Encourage the development of mental strength and creativity in the communities we belong to.
  • Demonstrate to communities on Sarmiento land that we are pushing for positive development.
  • Co-create culture-centered communities.
