January 10, 2026

This meeting marks a significant milestone in the partnership between the Ministry of Human Settlements and Development (MHSD) and the PS Group of Companies, specifically focused on the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) for a development project in Parang, Maguindanao (specifically mentioned as Barangay Sarmiento and Landasan).

The program was structured to ensure a smooth and meaningful flow of activities for the MOU Signing between the Ministry of Human Settlements and Development (MHSD) and the PS Group of Companies. It began with an opening prayer led by MHSD Division Chief, SHALAHUDDIN COSAIN, followed by the singing of the National Anthem and the BARMM Hymn, which set a solemn and formal tone for the event. This was followed by welcome remarks from PS Group of Companies President, MARIO PAOLO ANGELO S. MARCOS III, and opening remarks from MHSD Director II, SALEM C. DEMUNA, who highlighted the purpose and importance of the partnership. An inspirational message was then delivered by MHSD Director General, ESMAEL W. EBRAHIM, to emphasize the significance of collaboration in promoting sustainable development. The highlight of the program was the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding, which symbolized the official commitment of both parties. The event concluded with a message of commitment from FLP Construction Chief Operating Officer, OSCAR "JUN" SARMIENTO, JR., and closing remarks by MHSD Provincial Director, BAI MAROUDZ IBRAHIM, which formally wrapped up the ceremony.

## Key Themes and Highlights

### The "Four Cs" of the Partnership

The speakers emphasized four pillars that define this collaboration:

- Communication: Continuous dialogue between the private sector, the local government (Mayor's office), and the Ministry to refine plans.
- Collaboration: Moving beyond individual efforts to a collective contribution for the welfare of the community.
- Commitment: Honoring the hard work and perseverance that led to this signing ceremony.
- Community: Redefining "community" not just as a physical space or subdivision, but as a group of people who share common goals and agree to "grow together."

### PS Group's Vision and Legacy

The third-generation leaders of the PS Group (descendants of Pablo S. Sarmiento) shared their mission to continue the family legacy.

- Historical Roots: The Sarmiento Group was instrumental in the early development of Barangay Sarmiento, where businesses and communities once thrived.
- New Vision: They aim to be pioneers in sustainable and environmentally friendly communities and socially relevant businesses that promote inclusion.

### The "BANWAG" Structure

A centerpiece of the discussion was a structure called "BANWAG", which means "to light up" or "illuminate".

- It is intended to serve as a symbol of hope and a peaceful gathering place for people to find solutions and build together.

### Technical and Regional Development Goals

Director Suhaib A. Wahab of the MHSD highlighted the technical significance of the collaboration:

- Master Planning: The MHSD is working closely with the PS Group to ensure developments are high-class and sustainable.
- Economic Potential: There is a strategic focus on the Freeport and the entry of the BIMP-EAGA (Brunei-Indonesia-Malaysia-Philippines East ASEAN Growth Area) trade, positioning the area as a hub for barter trading and international commerce.
- Local Materials: The Ministry expressed a desire to showcase the use of local materials in housing and construction to promote Filipino craftsmanship on an international stage.

## Conclusion

The event concluded with the official signing of the MOU and a photo opportunity, signaling the start of a long-term commitment to nation-building and urban development in the region.
