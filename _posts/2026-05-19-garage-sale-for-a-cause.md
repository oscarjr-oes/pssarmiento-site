Education is the foundation of opportunity - and at PS Family, we believe every student deserves the chance to pursue their dreams. That's why we launched our Garage Sale for a Cause, a heartfelt initiative dedicated to raising funds for the SCHOLARS of Bulacan State University - Sarmiento Campus.

Location: First Sarmiento Property Holdings, Inc. Office, Block 1 Lot 36 University Heights Subdivision, Brgy. Kaypian, City of San Jose del Monte, Bulacan

## Community Impact So Far

Thanks to the generosity of our PS Family Members (Donors) and Buyers, in just two months our community has already made a difference. As of May 2026, we have successfully raised and deposited a total of ₱19,230 directly to Bulacan State University – Sarmiento Campus.

This achievement was made possible not only through financial contributions but also through the heartfelt support of buyers who donated their own preloved items.

## Why This Cause Matters

Each PESO raised and each item donated goes directly to supporting scholars who are striving to complete their education. By participating, you're not just giving away unused belongings—you're investing in the future of young minds who will one day lead and uplift our communities.

## A Call to All PS Family Members

We warmly invite more PS Family members to join us in this mission. Donors and buyers alike are welcome to support the program—whether through financial contributions, donating items, or simply spreading the word. Together, we can make education more accessible and empower students to achieve their goals.

## Continuing the Journey

This is only the beginning. The Garage Sale for a Cause will continue throughout the year and in the years ahead, creating a lasting legacy of generosity and hope. Together, we can make education more accessible and empower scholars to reach their dreams.

Be part of this movement. Support the Garage Sale for a Cause—and let's turn preloved items into priceless opportunities for the future!!
