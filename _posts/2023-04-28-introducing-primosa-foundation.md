## About the Foundation

In the tradition of the entrepreneurial Sarmientos, service to others has always been at the core of their values.

In this 3rd generation of the family, they have chosen to express this by taking care of the communities around them through providing access to tools and education as well as creating opportunities for entrepreneurial success.

Entrepreneurial success is a good measure of how much value you add to the lives of others.

Being in a position to give back, they are excited to create win-win opportunities for themselves and the communities they belong to.

## Goals & Values

- To improve communities that PS Group is a part of through sustainable development, access to tools and education, skills development, and creation of opportunities for entrepreneurship
- To create fertile ground not just for job creation, but entrepreneurship within the community that will create more jobs through businesses owned by community members
- To encourage the development of mental strength and creativity in the communities we belong to
- To demonstrate to communities on Sarmiento land that we are pushing for positive development
- Co-creating culture-centered communities
