First Sarmiento Property Holdings, Inc. is pleased to announce the successful grand opening and blessing of its new office, located at Block 1, Lot 1, University Square Subdivision, Barangay Kaypian, City of San Jose del Monte, Bulacan.

This new office stands as a testament to the continued growth of First Sarmiento Property Holdings, Inc., building upon the enduring legacy of the Pablo Sarmiento Group of Companies, an institution that has served Philippine communities for over seventy-five years.

**Building Overview**

The new facility has been thoughtfully designed to accommodate both business operations and community services across three floors.

*Ground Floor.* The Ground floor features two commercial spaces intended for lease. *Second Floor.* The second floor serves as the primary working area for the employees of First Sarmiento Property Holdings, Inc., and houses the office of the Board of Directors.

*Third Floor.* The third floor comprises a storage facility and a planned rental space intended for use as a function or meeting room, to be made available for lease in the future.

First Sarmiento Property Holdings, Inc. extends its sincere gratitude to all who participated in this celebration. The company looks forward to continuing its service to the community of San Jose del Monte and to fostering lasting relationships in the years ahead.

## 3. PS Group meeting with Cotabato State University Heads
**Author:** admin · **Category:** News & Events · **Published:** Feb 12
*(Re-verified 2026-08-04 in the Wix editor — this post was already complete on first pass; no collapsed sections.)*

January 10, 2026

FISHERIES AND INNOVATION
Focus: Sustainable Aquaculture and Institutional Partnerships

*(Post contains an embedded video)*

"This meaningful contribution has greatly strengthened fisheries research, instruction, and sustainable development." — University President, Sema G. Dilna, EdD, Cotabato State University.

**Spotlight: The Shift to Salt-Tolerant Tilapia**

While tilapia in the Philippines is traditionally a freshwater staple, a groundbreaking shift is occurring. Following the successful ventures into King Crab cultivation, the focus is now turning toward the UP SALT Tilapia (Saline-tolerant Tilapia).

- The Project: A 3-year initiative aimed at perfecting the "albino-looking" salt-tolerant breed.
- The Goal: To expand tilapia production into brackish and coastal waters, increasing food security and providing farmers with more versatile options.
- Why it matters: This breed adapts to higher salinity, making it resilient against changing water conditions and opening up new coastal "demo farm" opportunities.

**Partnership Highlights: A Milestone for Cotabato State University**

In a significant move for regional research, a Certificate of Appreciation was recently presented to the PS Group of Companies for their generous land donation.

This land donation has officially enabled the establishment of the Demo Farm Fishpond Fisheries Research Center, creating a hub for students and researchers to study fingerling development and sustainable harvesting techniques.

**From Pond to Table**

The research isn't just about biology; it's about the community. Recent activities at the demo farm included:

- Harvesting Success: Successful trial harvests (averaging 3kg per batch).
- Product Development: Exploring "Luto na" (Ready-to-eat) tilapia products and innovative concepts like "Tilapia Donut."
- Future Tech: Looking into pregnancy-safe nutritional drinks and the integration of fingerling distribution programs.

**Broader Impact: Beyond the Fishpond**

The UP SALT Tilapia initiative is more than a research project — it is a movement toward resilience and empowerment. The program aims to:

Contribute to Food Security: Strengthening the Philippines' supply of affordable protein through adaptable aquaculture.

Enhance Academic Excellence: Providing students and researchers with hands-on opportunities in fisheries science, sustainability, and product innovation.

Empower Communities: Creating livelihood options for coastal farmers and entrepreneurs through demo farms and ready-to-eat product development.

Advance Sustainable Practices: Integrating research with responsible resource management to ensure long-term ecological balance.

This milestone marks a convergence of science, education, and community — transforming the humble tilapia into a symbol of innovation and resilience.

With the land secured and the salt-tolerant tilapia project underway, the future of aquaculture in Cotabato looks bright.

## 4. MOU Signing: FLP and MHSD
**Author:** admin · **Category:** News & Events · **Published:** Feb 9 · **Featured**

January 10, 2026

This meeting marks a significant milestone in the partnership between the Ministry of Human Settlements and Development (MHSD) and the PS Group of Companies, specifically focused on the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) for a development project in Parang, Maguindanao (specifically mentioned as Barangay Sarmiento and Landasan).

The program was structured to ensure a smooth and meaningful flow of activities for the MOU Signing between the Ministry of Human Settlements and Development (MHSD) and the PS Group of Companies. It began with an opening prayer led by MHSD Division Chief, SHALAHUDDIN COSAIN, followed by the singing of the National Anthem and the BARMM Hymn, which set a solemn and formal tone for the event. This was followed by welcome remarks from PS Group of Companies President, MARIO PAOLO ANGELO S. MARCOS III, and opening remarks from MHSD Director II, SALEM C. DEMUNA, who highlighted the purpose and importance of the partnership. An inspirational message was then delivered by MHSD Director General, ESMAEL W. EBRAHIM, to emphasize the significance of collaboration in promoting sustainable development. The highlight of the program was the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding, which symbolized the official commitment of both parties. The event concluded with a message of commitment from FLP Construction Chief Operating Officer, OSCAR "JUN" SARMIENTO, JR., and closing remarks by MHSD Provincial Director, BAI MAROUDZ IBRAHIM, which formally wrapped up the ceremony.

*(Post contains an embedded video and an infographic-style program schedule image)*

**KEY THEMES AND HIGHLIGHTS**

**The "Four Cs" of the Partnership**

The speakers emphasized four pillars that define this collaboration:

- Communication: Continuous dialogue between the private sector, the local government (Mayor's office), and the Ministry to refine plans.
- Collaboration: Moving beyond individual efforts to a collective contribution for the welfare of the community.
- Commitment: Honoring the hard work and perseverance that led to this signing ceremony.
- Community: Redefining "community" not just as a physical space or subdivision, but as a group of people who share common goals and agree to "grow together."

**PS Group's Vision and Legacy**

The third-generation leaders of the PS Group (descendants of Pablo S. Sarmiento) shared their mission to continue the family legacy.

- Historical Roots: The Sarmiento Group was instrumental in the early development of Barangay Sarmiento, where businesses and communities once thrived.
- New Vision: They aim to be pioneers in sustainable and environmentally friendly communities and socially relevant businesses that promote inclusion.

**The "BANWAG" Structure**

A centerpiece of the discussion was a structure called "BANWAG", which means "to light up" or "illuminate".

- It is intended to serve as a symbol of hope and a peaceful gathering place for people to find solutions and build together.

**Technical and Regional Development Goals**

Director Suhaib A. Wahab of the MHSD highlighted the technical significance of the collaboration:

- Master Planning: The MHSD is working closely with the PS Group to ensure developments are high-class and sustainable.
- Economic Potential: There is a strategic focus on the Freeport and the entry of the BIMP-EAGA (Brunei-Indonesia-Malaysia-Philippines East ASEAN Growth Area) trade, positioning the area as a hub for barter trading and international commerce.
- Local Materials: The Ministry expressed a desire to showcase the use of local materials in housing and construction to promote Filipino craftsmanship on an international stage.

**Conclusion**

The event concluded with the official signing of the MOU and a photo opportunity, signaling the start of a long-term commitment to nation-building and urban development in the region.
