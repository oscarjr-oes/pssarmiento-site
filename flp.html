Applications of FLP Construction's Topographic Aerial LiDAR Services

  • Mapping and Surveying: Accurate elevation data, contours, and terrain features for urban planning, infrastructure development, and land management.
  • Floodplain Mapping: Identifying flood-prone areas, assessing risks, and developing flood mitigation strategies.
  • Forestry and Vegetation Analysis: Monitoring tree height, canopy structure, biomass, and density for forest inventory and habitat assessment.
  • Coastal Zone Mapping: Monitoring erosion, shoreline changes, and coastal habitats for effective coastal management.
  • Infrastructure Planning: Precise elevation data for aligning and engineering transportation networks.
  • Urban Planning and 3D City Modeling: Detailed 3D models for urban planning, simulations, and visualization.
  • Natural Hazard Assessment: Identifying at-risk areas, analyzing slope stability, and mapping fault lines for hazard mitigation.
