First Sarmiento Property Holdings, Inc.

FSPHI

Founded in December 1994, FSPHI has grown into a trusted name in real estate development, with its principal office in San Jose del Monte, Bulacan — property development and management across University Heights and beyond.

Real estate development is more than building houses or commercial spaces — it is shaping environments that inspire belonging and progress. FSPHI's mission is to provide affordable, well-designed, and strategically located developments that meet the needs of modern Filipino families, guided by integrity, excellence, and customer satisfaction.

Our services

Real estate development

From residential subdivisions to large-scale housing projects, blending comfort, accessibility, and value.

Property acquisition & subdivision development

Transforming raw land into thriving neighborhoods, compliant and sustainably planned.

Buy-and-sell of real estate

Transparent, beneficial property transactions for buyers and sellers alike.

Commercial leasing

Rental spaces in Muzon, Gaya-gaya, Poblacion, and Kaypian supporting local enterprise.

Projects

Over three decades, FSPHI has built a portfolio of developments across Luzon and Mindanao, including Danarra Condominium (Quezon City), Luxville Subdivision — a 15-hectare, hundreds-of-families housing community (Davao City), San Jose del Monte Cemetery Park (Bulacan), and government infrastructure projects in Mindanao. In San Jose del Monte, Bulacan, FSPHI developed Verde Heights Subdivision (15 hectares, 1,587 homes) and University Heights Subdivision (28 hectares, 279 lots) — both completed and fully sold — along with University Residences at San Jose.

Current project

University Heights Subdivision
